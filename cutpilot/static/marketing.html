WHAT CUTPILOT DOES

Less spreadsheet work. More control over stock.

Useful daily tools for independent butcher shops, built around the sales data they already have.

Simple data imports

Upload your product catalogue once, then bring in sales exports from your existing till.

Sales and gross-profit view

See revenue, product trends and estimated gross profit using the costs recorded for products.

Stock visibility

Track on-hand quantities, low-stock warnings, deliveries and recorded waste.

Buying suggestions

Review suggested order quantities based on sales history, stock and supplier lead time.

Business assistant

Ask questions about the shop's recorded sales, inventory and purchase recommendations.

Supplier orders

Prepare a purchase order for review. The owner remains in control of sending it to suppliers.
