Privacy notice

Information the service may process

Account name and email; business name, country and currency; product catalogue, inventory, sales imports, supplier records, purchase orders, waste records and audit information entered by the business.

Why information is used

To provide the business workspace, calculate sales and stock reports, prepare forecasts and buying suggestions, support account security, and provide customer support.

Separation and access

The hosted pilot creates a separate data file for each business workspace. The operator and authorized support personnel may still have infrastructure-level access. Never describe this as end-to-end encrypted or inaccessible to the service operator.

Retention, export and deletion

The service operator must define how long uploaded source files, account records, backups and deleted-workspace data are retained, and provide a practical export and deletion process.
