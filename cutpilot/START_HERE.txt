CUTPILOT v1.8 — START HERE

LOCAL PRODUCT DEMO:
  py -m pip install -r requirements.txt
  py app.py
  Open http://127.0.0.1:8765

LOCAL TEST OF INDIVIDUAL BUTCHER ACCOUNTS:
  PowerShell, in this folder:
    $env:CUTPILOT_MODE = "saas"
    $env:CUTPILOT_HOST = "127.0.0.1"
    $env:CUTPILOT_DATA_DIR = "$PWD\saas-test-data"
    $env:CUTPILOT_REQUIRE_EMAIL_VERIFICATION = "0"
    $env:CUTPILOT_COOKIE_SECURE = "0"
    py app.py
  Then open http://127.0.0.1:8765/signup
  Create two different test accounts and verify their products stay separate.

PUBLIC URLS IN SAAS MODE:
  /        public website
  /pricing public plans
  /signup  owner registration
  /login   sign-in
  /app     protected business dashboard

IMPORTANT:
  - Account creation works only when CUTPILOT_MODE=saas.
  - Public signup requires SMTP email settings and CUTPILOT_BASE_URL when email verification is on.
  - Checkout is NOT integrated. Pricing is proposed and no payment is taken.
  - render.yaml is an early single-instance pilot setup with a persistent disk.
  - Do not accept paying customer data until security, backup/restore and deployment review is complete.
  - See docs/HOSTED_SAAS_SETUP.md and docs/DEMO_AND_HOSTING_QUICKSTART.md.
